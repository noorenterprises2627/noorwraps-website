About us

Wholesale TPU sheets, mobile skins & Wotaer plotters in Mumbai

Noor Wraps (Noor Enterprises) is a wholesale supplier in Mumbai Central of cutting plotters, mobile skins, TPU membrane sheets and accessories for mobile skins and wraps.

We stock Noor TPU membrane sheets and original Mietubl TPU membrane sheets (hydrogel screen guards, screen protectors and back films) in clear, matte, privacy, UV, auto-repair, bulletproof and iron film types. Noor TPU membrane sheets stick easily even on the matte backs of new Android phones and iPhones. Big size hydrogel sheets are also available for tablets and laptops.

We are a supplier of Wotaer cutting plotters, including the Wotaer ND-390 and Wotaer F7-Pro screen guard and mobile skin cutting machines. They cut up to 19 inches, come with laptop designs, support YouTube & Instagram, have an in-built display and Bluetooth, and carry a 1 year warranty.

We also supply mobile skins (embossed, sparkle, transparent and textured), skin rolls, UV printers, UV vacuum machines, heat guns, heat pens, and plotter accessories including plotter blades and blade holders (plotter heads) for Wotaer and Mietubl plotters, plotter mats and glue pads.

Frequently asked questions
Do you sell retail or wholesale?

Noor Wraps is wholesale only. Message us on WhatsApp for wholesale rates, or visit our shop opposite Mumbai Central Railway Station (East).

Which TPU membrane sheet sticks on matte-back phones?

Noor TPU membrane sheets are 4-layer sheets (120 × 190 mm) that stick easily even on the matte backs of new Android phones and iPhones, with almost no centre-line issue after application.

Which TPU / hydrogel screen guard types are available?

Regular, privacy, auto-repair, bulletproof, iron film, UV, UV silicone, UV 9H and UV privacy, in clear and matte, from Noor and original Mietubl. Big size sheets from 11 to 19 inch are also available for tablets and laptops.

Which mobile skins and rolls do you stock?

Imported 3-layer embossed and textured skins, premium embossed, sparkle, full UV embossed and transparent skins, plus printed, embossed, textured, plain and leather skin rolls for phones, laptops, tablets, AirPods, cameras and gaming consoles.

Do you sell Wotaer cutting plotters?

Yes. We supply Wotaer ND-390 and Wotaer F7-Pro cutting plotters for cutting TPU screen guards, back films and mobile skins. Both cut up to 19 inches, include laptop designs, support YouTube & Instagram, have an in-built display and Bluetooth, and come with a 1 year warranty. Wotaer plotter blades and blade holders are also available.

Do you sell plotter blades, blade holders, mats and glue pads?

Yes. We supply plotter blades and blade holders (plotter heads) for Wotaer and Mietubl plotters, including Mietubl blade sets (5 pc & 10 pc) and blade holders for 180T / 180T Pro and M180T, plus plotter mats (with or without glue) and A4 & A3 glue pads.

Which other machines and tools do you sell?

The CH-03 flatbed UV printer for custom covers, M10 and UV + Vacuum machines for curing UV membrane sheets, the AI Pro Box phone holder, heat guns and heat pens, plus Mietubl plotter blades, blade holders, glue pads and mats.
